Token by Token is an inference lab for asking a harder question: what do serving measurements actually prove? At sixteen simulated users, the recorded SGLang deployment delivered twenty-seven point five percent more output throughput than vLLM. Yet both missed the declared slow-tail decode floor. Episode zero found another trap: the same token cap did not guarantee equal delivered work. These are tested closed-loop loads, not production capacity.

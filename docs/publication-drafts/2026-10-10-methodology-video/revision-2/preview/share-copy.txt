Token by Token is an open inference lab, and every token is a measurement. A benchmark number means little until the system, workload, scenario, metric rules, validity gates and evidence boundary are declared. This introduction covers what enters the lab (real chat, agentic coding with tools, tool gaps and human think time, plus optional issue-fix tasks, synthetic agent replay and tool-agent-user sessions), how load is replayed, what is measured, and where the evidence stops: work before speed, validity before capacity, observed, not inferred.

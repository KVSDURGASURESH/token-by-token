# Owner-approved permanent video direction

- Product descriptor and opening: **The Inference Lab**. Narration: "Token by Token is the inference lab". Do not call it "an inference lab" or "an open inference lab" in the opening visuals or narration.
- Use exact dashboard logo, wordmark, font and dark palette.
- Blend dashboard captures into the canvas; avoid screenshot boxes and rigid grey/white separators. Alternate real UI actions with deliberate typography and animation.
- Include a real configuration-note unfold interaction when relevant. Show public source UI, never invented configuration values.
- Penultimate scene: prominent real dashboard action for the specific subject. Methodology video scrolls down the methodology page; episode videos demonstrate the respective episode with an appropriate action.
- End card: subject-relevant measurement or a catchy evidence-safe benchmarking quote, exact logo/wordmark, GitHub link, dashboard portal link when available, and a scannable portal QR. Verify destination availability; local review drafts may include the owner-requested URL with verification explicitly pending.
- Maintain approved music and narration dominance; sync motion accents to musical cues without sacrificing reading time.
- Dashboard scrolls always move naturally downward. Use a fixed camera and one monotonic page scroll; avoid adding a reversing pan on top of scrolling footage. Capture at the delivery frame rate, keep settled UI/text fixed, and remove unintended vertical drift, oscillation, scale breathing and frame-rate conversion judder.
